About Kestrel Drone

A free airspace and preflight app for drone pilots, by Kestrel Aviation Solutions.

How LAANC works here

Kestrel Drone is not a LAANC provider. It shows where LAANC is needed and the highest altitude LAANC can approve in each grid square. When you ask for authorization, it opens the FAA-approved provider you choose in More, and your request goes through that provider.

Where the data comes from

Airspace and restrictions

The FAA: controlled airspace, UAS facility map ceilings, security restrictions, prohibited and restricted areas, stadiums, FRIAs, recreational flying sites and airports.

TFRs and NOTAMs

TFRs from tfr.faa.gov. NOTAMs from the NOTAM Management Service.

National parks

Park boundaries from the National Park Service.

Weather

The National Weather Service and Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com, CC BY 4.0). The Kp index from NOAA's Space Weather Prediction Center. Sun times are calculated on your device.

Maps

CARTO and OpenStreetMap contributors, USGS and Esri.

Your data

Your flights, drones and checklist stay on your device. There is no account.

Questions and problems

Report a problem or ask for a feature on GitHub. Kestrel Drone is made by Kestrel Aviation Solutions, who also make Kestrel EFB for pilots.
